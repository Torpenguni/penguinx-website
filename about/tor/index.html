Founder · Profile

Thanapong "Tor" Vongchinsri

Thanapong "Tor" Vongchinsri is the founder and CEO of PenguinX, Thailand's F&B ecosystem builder. He has 13 years in the food and beverage industry, and PenguinX grew out of Torpenguin, the media channel he started for restaurant owners.

What he works on

At PenguinX, Tor leads the four parts of the ecosystem that connect brands and suppliers with Thai restaurant owners.

  • Media: Torpenguin and an in-house studio that produce content for restaurant owners.
  • Event: conferences and trade shows organized by PenguinX, including TRC, Restech, CafCon and DAMN Expo.
  • Education: restaurant business courses, including the Restaurant MBA program.
  • Solution: strategy, branding and business development for F&B brands.

Credentials
