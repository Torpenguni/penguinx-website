Founder · Profile

Thanapong "Tor" Vongchinsri

Thanapong "Tor" Vongchinsri, known as "Torpenguin", is the founder and CEO of PenguinX, Thailand's F&B ecosystem builder. He has more than 13 years of experience in design, marketing, branding and restaurant management, and PenguinX grew out of Torpenguin, the media channel he started for restaurant owners.

Contacttor@penguinx.co

What I believe

I'm not the best at what I do. But I believe you don't have to wait until you're the best to be useful to others.

And when our business and society become one and the same, every bit of growth in our business means society grows along with us.
