01Who we are

Penguin X Co., Ltd. ("PenguinX", "we", "us") is a restaurant ecosystem builder based in Bangkok, Thailand. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy, which means we decide why and how it is used.

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to tor@penguinx.co. See section 12 for full contact details.

02Data we collect

Information you give us

When you fill in the "Book a free 30-minute call" form, email us, call us or message us, we may collect:

  • Contact details: your name, email address, phone number or LINE ID.
  • Business details: your company or brand name, business type, the services you are interested in and, if you choose to share it, your budget range.
  • Your message: what you tell us about your goals, and any later correspondence with us.

Name and email are required on the form so that we can reply. All other fields are optional. Please do not send us sensitive personal data (such as health, religious or biometric data); we do not need it to answer your enquiry.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical and usage data: pages visited, the page you sent the form from, your language setting, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site.
  • Interaction events: whether you submitted the form or clicked our email, phone or LINE links. We record that a click happened, not the content of any message you then send.
  • Your choices on this site: your cookie consent choice and your preferred language, saved in your browser's local storage.

Cookie-based analytics only run if you accept them. See section 7.

07Cookies and analytics

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept cookies for measurement. Until you accept, Google Analytics runs in a restricted mode: it sets no analytics or advertising cookies and receives only basic, cookieless signals, which Google uses to estimate overall traffic.

  • If you accept: Google Analytics 4 sets cookies (such as _ga) to count visits and see which pages and campaigns lead to enquiries. Google Ads may use the same data to measure the results of our advertising.
  • If you decline: no analytics or advertising cookies are set.
  • Always on: your cookie choice and language preference are kept in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them. This data stays on your device.

08How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a project: up to 2 years from our last contact with you, so we can pick up the conversation if you come back. You can ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Client records: for the length of our business relationship, and afterwards for as long as Thai accounting, tax or other laws require (generally up to 10 years).
  • Analytics data: for the retention period set in Google Analytics, no longer than 14 months.

When the period ends, we delete the data or make it anonymous.

09How we protect it

The site is served only over encrypted HTTPS. Form submissions go straight to our inbox and are not stored in a public database. We limit access to staff who need it and use providers with recognised security practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so if we become aware of a breach that risks your rights, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Committee and, where required, you, as the PDPA requires.

10Your rights

Under the PDPA, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data and get a copy of it, and ask how we obtained it.
  • Data portability: receive your data in a commonly used electronic format, or ask us to send it to another organisation where technically possible.
  • Correct data that is inaccurate, out of date or incomplete.
  • Erase or anonymise your data when we no longer have a lawful reason to keep it.
  • Restrict how we use your data, for example while we check a correction request.
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to any direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for example through Cookie settings. This does not affect processing that happened before you withdrew it.
  • Complain to the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC) of Thailand, at www.pdpc.or.th.

To use any of these rights, email tor@penguinx.co with the subject "Privacy request". We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting. We will respond within 30 days and will not charge you unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive. If we cannot fulfil a request, for example because the law requires us to keep the data, we will explain why.

11Children

This website and our services are intended for businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 20, the age of legal majority in Thailand. If you believe a minor has sent us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

12Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our services, tools or the law change. We will post the new version on this page with a new "Last updated" date and, for significant changes, tell people who have an open conversation with us.
