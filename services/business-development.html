Business Development — strategic partnerships and expansion

Strategic partnerships, franchising frameworks, and expansion roadmaps for F&B brands ready to scale.

Growth in F&B is rarely a marketing problem alone. It's a partnership problem, a format problem, a franchise problem, an expansion-readiness problem. PenguinX's business development service treats it that way — strategic, not tactical.

What this service covers

Senior advisory and execution support on the commercial decisions that shape the next phase of an F&B business. We work alongside founder and leadership teams on strategic partnerships, franchise frameworks, and expansion roadmaps.

  • Strategic partnership strategy and deal structuring
  • Franchise model design and operator playbooks
  • Multi-brand and multi-format expansion planning
  • International expansion advisory for Thai F&B brands going abroad
  • Inbound brand entry into the Thai market

Who this is for

F&B brands at an inflection point — about to franchise, about to enter a new market, about to launch a second concept. Or international brands trying to enter Thailand and looking for a partner who understands the operator landscape.

Why we're different

Tor and the PenguinX leadership team have done this work as operators, advisors, and the people on the other side of the deal. We bring both the industry network and the operating perspective that makes partnership conversations move faster.
