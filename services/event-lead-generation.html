Event Lead Generation — industry gatherings that convert

Industry events designed to fill pipelines and unlock partnerships — not just attendance counts.

Industry events generate leads — when they're built around the audience, not the sponsor list. PenguinX runs the most operator-trusted event calendar in Thai F&B, and we turn that calendar into measurable pipeline for the brands that need to be in front of these audiences.

What this service covers

Lead generation through industry-event participation, from format selection to on-floor execution to post-event follow-up. We use our own event properties — TRC, Damn Expo, Restech, CafCon, #LongKaek — and we run custom event activations when the brief calls for one.

  • Event sponsorship strategy across PenguinX-owned properties
  • Branded session, panel, and demo design
  • On-floor activations and lead-capture mechanics
  • Pre-event audience targeting and invitation flows
  • Post-event qualification and handoff to client sales teams

Who this is for

B2B brands selling into the F&B industry — equipment makers, technology platforms, ingredient suppliers, financial services, and the platforms that serve restaurants directly. Anyone whose buyer is a restaurant owner or operator.

Why we're different

Most agencies sell event participation. We own the events. Clients pay for sponsorship and get the audience, the format design, and the editorial amplification that surrounds the event — bundled.
