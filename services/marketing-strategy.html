Marketing Strategy — built for F&B unit economics

Data-driven growth plans tuned to the real margins, traffic patterns, and AOV dynamics of restaurant operations.

F&B is its own discipline. The unit economics, the margin structure, the way a single menu change ripples through prep, labor, and waste — none of it matches the standard consumer-marketing playbook. PenguinX builds marketing strategies that respect how restaurants actually run.

What this service covers

Full-stack marketing strategy for F&B operators and the brands that serve them. We start with the business model, then build a marketing plan that maps to real margins, real traffic patterns, and real AOV mechanics — not benchmarks borrowed from other categories.

  • Market and competitive analysis
  • Customer segmentation and channel strategy
  • Pricing and menu engineering inputs
  • Annual marketing planning with measurable KPIs
  • Channel-specific tactical playbooks

Who this is for

Restaurant groups planning expansion, F&B brands looking to professionalize their go-to-market, and the senior leadership teams who need a marketing plan that holds up in the boardroom and in the kitchen.

Why we're different

Our strategy work is built by people who've operated restaurants and run multi-million-dollar campaigns inside the industry. We can hold both conversations — the unit-economics one and the brand-equity one — in the same meeting.
