Corporate Training & Public Speaking

In-house workshops and industry keynotes from Tor and the PenguinX leadership team for F&B operators and corporate audiences.

The PenguinX leadership team spends most of its time inside the F&B industry — operating, advising, and listening. The training and public speaking service brings that experience into other companies' rooms.

What this service covers

Two formats, same source material.

Corporate training

In-house workshops for F&B teams — from front-of-house staff training to senior leadership offsites. We build the curriculum around the business, not around a generic playbook. Sessions are tailored to the brand, the team, and the operational problems they're actually facing.

Public speaking

Tor and the PenguinX leadership team speak at industry conferences, brand events, and partner summits across Asia. Talks range from operator-level tactical sessions to keynote-format addresses on the future of the F&B industry.

Who this is for

Restaurant groups training their teams. Suppliers and platforms equipping their merchant-facing staff. Industry conferences looking for speakers who can hold a room of operators. Corporate brands running F&B-focused internal programs.

Why we're different

Training programs are a side effect of running an active F&B media and education business. The content is fresh because we use it every week — and the speakers are doing the work, not just talking about it.
