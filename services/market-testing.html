02 / Why it matters

Thai kitchens decide differently.

Restaurant owners in Thailand judge a product by taste, price per serving, pack size and how it fits their menu. What sells at home does not always fit here, and local tastes vary between regions.

A small test with real operators shows you what to change in the product, price or message before you invest in distribution and a full launch.

05 / FAQ

Questions brands ask.

What can we learn from a market test?

How restaurant owners react to your product's taste, price, pack size and message, and which types of restaurant are most interested. You learn this before committing to distribution and a full launch.

Do we need a Thai distributor first?

Not always. Many brands test first to learn whether the market is worth entering, then look for partners. Bringing in products for sampling may still need to meet Thai import and FDA rules, so check this early.

How do you collect feedback?

Through conversations with owners who try your product at our events and in our community, and through how owners respond to Torpenguin content.

How long does a test take?

It depends on the format. Sampling follows the date of the event, and a media test can run in a few weeks. We set the timeline together.

What happens after the test?

If the results are good, you can move straight into launch through the same channels: booths, content and introductions to distributors or partners.
