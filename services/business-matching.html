Business Matching — connect brands that move the needle

Bring brands, suppliers, and operators together with intent and structure — turning chance encounters into commercial outcomes.

The F&B industry runs on relationships, but most of them happen by accident. Business matching is the discipline of making those relationships happen by design — connecting brands, suppliers, and operators who should be talking but haven't found each other yet.

What this service covers

Curated B2B introductions and structured matchmaking inside the PenguinX network. We use our editorial intelligence and event programming to connect the right counterparties at the right moment, with the right framing.

  • Operator-to-supplier matchmaking
  • Brand-to-distributor introductions
  • Strategic partnership facilitation
  • Curated meeting tracks at PenguinX events
  • Post-match follow-up and deal support

Who this is for

Brands looking for distribution. Suppliers looking for accounts. Operators looking for partners. Anyone in the F&B value chain who knows the meeting they need but doesn't have the introduction.

Why we're different

With over 200 brand partners and active relationships across the operator community, we already know who needs to meet whom. The matchmaking service formalizes what we do informally every week.
