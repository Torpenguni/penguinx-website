What TRC is

The Thailand Restaurant Conference (TRC) is PenguinX's flagship gathering for restaurant owners and senior operators. It runs as multi-day, multi-track programming built around the decisions owners actually face: costs and unit economics, delivery, staffing, menus, AI in day-to-day operations, investment and expansion.

The 2026 edition

TRC 2026 ran 27–29 August 2026 at IMPACT Exhibition Center, Hall 6, Muang Thong Thani, with 200+ booths, 40+ speakers and 25+ sessions. It ran alongside Restech, PenguinX's restaurant tech and equipment expo, in the neighbouring halls — so operators could hear the strategy in the conference rooms and compare the equipment on the expo floor in the same visit.

TRC 2025 in numbers

The 2025 edition brought together 4,500 restaurant owners over three days of programming with 45+ speakers. Read the TRC 2025 recap →

Who it is for

  • Restaurant owners and operators — from single-shop founders to multi-unit chains.
  • Food service suppliers and brands that want time with owners who make buying decisions — as exhibitors, sponsors or session partners.
