What CafCon is

CafCon — the Café Business Conference — is a ticketed conference and trade show built for people who run cafés as a business. It goes beyond coffee beans to what makes a café last: business models and day-to-day management, taught by leading speakers from the café industry, plus partners and solutions that help cafés raise their margins — ingredients, equipment and technology.

The first edition

CafCon's first edition ran 15–16 March 2026 at IMPACT Exhibition Center, Hall 7, Muang Thong Thani, with 4,513 participants and 50 exhibitors.

Who it is for

  • Café owners and would-be owners who want to run a café as a real business.
  • Roasters, ingredient, equipment and technology suppliers selling to cafés.
