What Restech is

Restech is PenguinX's expo for restaurant technology and equipment. Instead of brochures, the floor is built for live demos and side-by-side comparison, so restaurant owners can try systems and equipment and make purchasing decisions on the spot.

The 2026 edition

Restech 2026 — “Step Into The Future” — ran 27–29 August 2026 at IMPACT Exhibition Center, Hall 5–6, Muang Thong Thani, alongside the Thailand Restaurant Conference. Operators came for the conference and walked the expo floor in the same visit. On the evening of 27 August, a Networking Night brought together restaurant owners, property developers, investors, government agencies, banks and media.

On the floor

The expo is organised around what a restaurant needs to run: kitchen equipment, management technology, marketing, suppliers, financial systems and future restaurant models. Registration is free for restaurant owners.

A community-led expo

Restech draws on the PenguinX community — Torpenguin's 1.95M+ followers and a restaurant-owner network across 52 provinces — so the visitors are the operators who run restaurants, not general trade traffic.

Who it is for

  • Restaurant owners comparing POS, kitchen equipment, delivery and back-office tools.
  • Tech and equipment suppliers looking for restaurant buyers — including brands entering the Thai market.

To exhibit or apply for business matching, visit the Restech website or talk to the PenguinX team.
