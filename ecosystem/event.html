PenguinX Event — Industry gatherings that convene the F&B world

TRC, Damn Expo, Restech, CafCon, and #LongKaek — the events that bring together operators, suppliers, and brands across the F&B ecosystem.

Events are where the F&B industry actually convenes. Conferences, expos, peer dinners, networking forums — the moments when operators get into the same room, in person, and decide who they trust. PenguinX runs the most concentrated calendar of these moments in the country.

What sits under this pillar

Five flagship properties, each engineered for a specific audience and a specific commercial purpose. Together they reach over 10,000 industry decision-makers a year.

TRC — Thailand Restaurant Conference

The country's largest restaurant operator conference. Multi-day, multi-track programming for owners, managers, and senior operators. The room where decisions about the year ahead get made.

Damn Expo

The nightlife and craft-liquor industry's home expo. Bar owners, distributors, and beverage brands in one trade-show floor — B2B matchmaking built into the format.

Restech

Restaurant technology and equipment expo. Where suppliers and operators meet on the floor for live demos, comparison, and procurement conversations that move within the show.

CafCon

The café business conference. Coffee shop owners, roasters, and category suppliers — the segment of the industry that needs its own playbook and finally has it.

#LongKaek

The peer-hosted community event. Restaurant owners hosting other restaurant owners at their venues, across 45 provinces. Smallest format, deepest trust.

How it connects to the rest of the ecosystem

The Event pillar runs on audiences PenguinX Media has been building for years. Operators we feature on Torpenguin become attendees, speakers, and #LongKaek hosts. Education programs surface insights that drive conference themes. Solution-agency clients get curated brand presence inside formats their audience actually trusts.

Events feed the rest of the ecosystem just as much as they consume from it — earned content from every event runs back through Media, and the relationships built on-floor become the network that powers everything else.
