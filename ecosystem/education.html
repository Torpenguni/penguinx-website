Curriculum the industry didn't have.

Most operator education in Thailand came from two places: academic programs written by people who had never run a P&L, or vendor seminars built to sell something at the end. PenguinX Education exists because neither was good enough.

Each format is taught by operators who've made the mistakes — multi-unit founders, senior chefs, supply-chain leaders, marketers who've sat through real launch cycles. The case studies are local; the frameworks are tested; the peer relationships outlast the syllabus.

Graduates leave with two things: a clearer playbook, and a class full of operators they can call when the next decision shows up.
