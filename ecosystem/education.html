PenguinX Education — Knowledge that compounds across operators

Restaurant Business Academy, Restaurant MBA, and a growing body of books and content that elevate the entire F&B industry.

Knowledge compounds — and in an industry where most operators learn by trial and a lot of error, the compounding curve matters more than any single tactic. The Education pillar is where PenguinX invests in the long game: programs that lift the floor of the entire F&B industry.

What sits under this pillar

Restaurant Business Academy

Practical operator training across the disciplines that actually run a restaurant — unit economics, marketing, supply chain, team management. Built for owners who are scaling and managers who want to grow into ownership.

Restaurant MBA

The flagship long-form program. A serious business-school-style cohort for restaurant founders, multi-brand operators, and senior F&B leaders. The first program of its kind in Thailand purpose-built for the industry.

Books

Best-selling operator-facing books authored by Tor and the PenguinX team. Long-form knowledge that lives outside the social feed and ends up on every serious operator's shelf.

How it connects to the rest of the ecosystem

Education is the long-cycle counterpart to Media. Where Media reaches operators in their feed every day, Education locks in the relationships that matter most — the founders building the brands that will shape the next decade of Thai F&B.

Cohort graduates become Event speakers. Their stories become Media features. Their growing brands become Solution-agency clients. It's a loop, and Education is the part that turns audience into community.

"If you want to professionalize an industry, you start with the people running it. Education is the slowest channel and the most important one."
