PenguinX Media — storytelling that runs the F&B industry's conversation

Two houses, one editorial voice. Torpenguin and Tungtua reach more F&B operators in a week than most trade publications reach in a year — and they shape what restaurant owners actually talk about.

The Media pillar is where PenguinX starts every conversation. Long before a brand campaign goes live or an event opens its doors, the audience has already been built — through years of operator-first content that treats restaurant owners as the protagonists, not the targets.

What sits under this pillar

Media is run as a portfolio of operator-facing properties. Each has its own voice, audience, and content rhythm — but they all share the same editorial backbone.

Torpenguin

The flagship channel and the industry's largest F&B operator network. Daily content on running restaurants in Thailand — unit economics, marketing tactics, supplier deals, the human side of the business. This is where Tor's voice as the F&B industry's most-read founder-operator lives.

Tungtua

The expansion property. Where Torpenguin speaks to existing operators, Tungtua reaches the next layer — aspiring owners, F&B-curious consumers, and the broader food-business audience that surrounds the industry. Same standards, wider doorway.

How it connects to the rest of the ecosystem

Media isn't a standalone product — it's the amplifier that makes everything else work. Events get pre-built audiences. Education programs get qualified applicants. Solution agency clients get earned distribution that no paid campaign can buy.

It also runs the other direction. Operators we feature on Torpenguin become hosts at #LongKaek. Knowledge that surfaces in Education programs becomes content that loops back into the feed. The ecosystem feeds itself.

"If you want to reach Thai restaurant owners, you don't run ads at them — you talk to them like an insider. That's what PenguinX Media is."

Why brands work with us through Media

Brand partners come to PenguinX Media for one reason: trusted distribution. A feature on Torpenguin doesn't read like an ad — it reads like the industry's most-followed operator vouching for you, because it is.

  • Founder-led content with Tor
  • Long-form features on operator pages, brand stories, and product launches
  • Multi-platform amplification across owned channels
  • Co-created content with house brands during industry events
