How Lineman Wongnai drove nationwide brand activations with PenguinX

Inside the multi-channel campaign that took Lineman Wongnai's awareness and activation strategy to operators across all 77 provinces — and what F&B brands can learn from it.

When Lineman Wongnai approached PenguinX, the brief was deceptively simple: build awareness for new merchant features across Thailand's fragmented restaurant landscape. The challenge — reaching operators outside Bangkok at a meaningful scale — turned out to be anything but.

The challenge

Restaurant owners in Thailand's secondary cities consume content differently from Bangkok-based peers. Generic digital campaigns struggle to penetrate this audience. Lineman Wongnai needed a campaign that combined trusted peer endorsement, hyperlocal storytelling, and real-world brand activations — all without diluting the brand.

The approach

PenguinX leaned on the full ecosystem — Torpenguin's media reach, the Restaurant MBA network, and #LongKaek peer gatherings — to design a layered campaign that met restaurant owners where they already were.

  • Provincial chapter activations through #LongKaek host venues
  • Founder-led content with Tor reaching 2M+ F&B operators
  • Co-branded merchant onboarding workshops at Restaurant MBA
  • Targeted social activations during peak community events
"PenguinX didn't just run a campaign — they plugged us into the most engaged restaurant community in Thailand."

The outcome

Beyond the awareness lift, the partnership produced something rarer: a baseline of trust between Lineman Wongnai and operators in regions that historically resisted top-down brand activation. Lessons from this campaign now inform how PenguinX builds nationwide programs for other F&B brands.
