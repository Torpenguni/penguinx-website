Damn Expo returns: Thailand's nightlife industry comes together

The country's premier nightlife and craft liquor expo is back — bigger, louder, and with a renewed focus on B2B matchmaking for bar and venue operators.

Damn Expo — the country's premier nightlife and craft-liquor trade expo — is back for its next edition. After a sold-out first year, the show is expanding both its B2B matchmaking program and its on-floor brand presence.

What the show is

Damn Expo brings together the segment of the F&B industry that doesn't fit neatly into the restaurant-focused trade calendar: bar and venue operators, beverage brands, craft distilleries, and the nightlife supply chain. It's a trade show, but it runs more like a curated marketplace.

What's expanding this edition

  • A dedicated B2B matchmaking program for brands looking for bar and venue distribution
  • Expanded craft-liquor pavilion with international brand participation
  • On-floor masterclasses for bar staff and venue operators
  • Networking-focused programming designed around the deals that close at trade shows

Who should attend

Bar owners and operators scouting new products. Beverage brands looking for distribution. Distributors, importers, and the broader supply chain serving the nightlife industry. International brands evaluating market entry into Thailand.

"Damn Expo is the one show a year that's built the way bar owners actually buy — fast, hands-on, and built on relationships."
